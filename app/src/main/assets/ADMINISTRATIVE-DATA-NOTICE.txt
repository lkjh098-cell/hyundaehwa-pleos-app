The shared asset groups and simplifies boundaries for conversation-region selection.
It is not a live administrative register or turn-by-turn navigation boundary service.
Content provenance is stored separately on each topic. Preference and conversation cards are editorial questions with no correct answer; quiz fact sources and content tiers are identified in the asset.
No vehicle coordinates, private addresses or route histories are stored in this file.
